\paragraph{RQ-A (primary).} Among five preprocessing strategies for Indonesian \id{-nya} --- keep, naive regex strip, dictionary-aware Sastrawi clitic strip, sentinel mask, and oracle anaphora resolution on a held-out subset --- which yields the best retrieval effectiveness on MIRACL-id, and does the optimal strategy depend on retriever architecture (BM25 vs.\ BGE-m3)?

\paragraph{RQ-B (embedded).} Does anaphora-aware document preprocessing --- replacing anaphoric \id{-nya} with its resolved antecedent --- provide an upper bound on attainable improvement from \id{-nya}-aware preprocessing for Indonesian retrieval, and how large is that upper bound?
